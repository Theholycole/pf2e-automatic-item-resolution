# Consumables census

Generated by `tools/census.mjs` on 2026-10-17 from the PF2e system's packs (equipment). Rerun it after each batch; `reports/census.json` has every item's row. Each item is compiled with a stand-in owner (its own level, middling attribute modifiers, no feats), as live play passes the item's actor.

- **1724** consumables. **238** are the spell module's (scroll, wand, catalyst, spell-gem, anything with the catalyst trait, and what its claims() reads), so this module handles **1486**.
- **171** alchemical bombs are `weapon` items (group `bomb`), not consumables: PF2e Strikes with them and rolls their damage and splash. **164** get the module's card after PF2e's damage roll: **155** for the splash on the creatures within 5 feet of the target, **62** with riders read from their text ("On a hit … off-guard", "Frightened 2 on a critical hit").
- **2679** permanent items (equipment, weapons, armor, shields, ammunition, containers) have an "Activate": a later phase.

Results, for the items this module handles:

- **resolves**: a plan the core's families resolve at every degree with no gaps
- **partial**: the module applies part of what the text says and puts the rest on a note Effect with the item's text ("Effect + note", counted apart as the spell module counts its own)
- **note-only**: nothing the module reads but a time: a note Effect with the item's text for that time (counted apart, as the spell module counts its note-only spells)
- **gaps**: a plan the core resolves only in part (the gaps are listed below)
- **builtin**: an ability-module builtin (zone: a lasting area or a snare, its save plans the core's, placed by the ability module's zones; cast-spell: casts a spell, the spell module's; no-effect: the GM's)
- **none**: no plan yet
