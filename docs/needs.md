# What the items still need

Generated by `tools/needs.mjs` on 2026-10-25. Every consumable and permanent item activation whose card doesn't do all its text yet (its plan *partial*, *note only*, *nothing read*, or *the GM's* no-effect), sorted by what its card would need to do the rest. `reports/needs.json` has every PF2e entry with all its needs; the installed modules' items are counted here and named in `.cache/needs-third-party.json`, which git ignores.

How an item is sorted: the words its card leaves over (its notes, unread sentences, GM lines and note Effects; for one nothing reads, its own text) are matched against each need's words in `tools/needs-rules.mjs`. An item gets every need its words show. Its **main** need is the first in the table's order, the most particular first, so a Strike that also needs a creature summoned is listed under creatures. The sorting reads words, not rules, so an item can sit under a need it mentions only in passing.

- **PF2e:** 1448 entries (466 consumables, 982 activations): 687 nothing read, 337 partial, 147 the GM's, 277 note only.
- **Installed modules:** 130 entries (88 consumables, 42 activations): 51 partial, 30 nothing read, 46 note only, 3 the GM's.
