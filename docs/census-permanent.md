# Permanent items' activations: census

Generated by `tools/census-permanent.mjs` on 2026-10-16 from the PF2e system's equipment pack. `reports/census-permanent.json` has every activation's row. Activations are played live: the character sheet's Activate buttons post each one's card. `builtin:pf2e-rules` is ammunition whose own PF2e rules already add what it does to the Strike that fires it (its splash goes on the splash card after the damage roll).

- **2679** permanent items have an "Activate". **592** aren't read here: staves, wands and spellhearts, and the items the spell module's claims() reads (its casts, item spellshapes), and bombs (their own card). So **2087** are read: **2593** activations.
- **77** activations are etched runes' (Vorpal, Hauling, Bolka's Blessing): played from the weapon or armor the rune is etched onto, whose sheet row gets the rune's buttons (src/compiler/runes.js). **46** of them get a card.
- **1749** activations state a frequency ("once per day"), kept on the plan as `trigger.frequency`. **428** have a Trigger (a reaction or a free action).
